CS210 Programming Assignment 1 - Uno Turn Order
Boilerplate bundle

What's in here:

  Player.h   - the new T for this assignment, given in full, matches
               Section 2 of the assignment PDF exactly. Drop it into
               your project as-is.

  Stack.h    - the Stack<T> from the stacks and queues unit, only
               needed if you're attempting the extra credit
               (Section 9, Card + Stack<Card> hands). Drop it in next
               to your other headers if you don't already have it.

  main.cpp   - starter file for your main.cpp. Part 1 (the required
               test harness) is already written, don't modify it.
               Build your Uno scene (Part 2) below the marked comment,
               in the same file. See Section 8 of the assignment PDF
               for the exact expected output of Part 1 and the
               requirements for Part 2.

Not included on purpose:

  Card.h, and the addAnywhere / deleteAnywhere / reverse / concat
  implementations in ArrayList.h and LinkedList.h - those are the
  assignment. Section 4 through 7 of the PDF give you the signature
  and behavior spec for each, not the code.

You still need your own Node.h, List.h, ArrayList.h, LinkedList.h, and
Data.h from the refactor guide. This bundle only adds what's new for
this assignment.
